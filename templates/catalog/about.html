SOURCE AND METHOD

How product information is collected

Product records come from Open Food Facts, a community-maintained open food database. LabelLens stores the source product identifier and links to the original record so details can be checked against their source.

Product names, brands, categories, images, ingredients, declared allergens and nutrition values are shown only when supplied by the source. Nutrition amounts retain their reported units and basis.

Open Food Facts data is provided under the Open Database License (ODbL). Product images can carry separate licensing conditions. See the source terms and attribution information. LabelLens is an independent project.

COMPARISON RULES

How to read a comparison

  • Values are compared only when nutrient, unit and measurement basis match.
  • Per 100 g, per 100 ml and per serving stay separate.
  • A per-serving amount is calculated only when the source provides a serving size with a compatible unit. It is marked as calculated.
  • Missing information is labeled “Not reported”; it is never treated as zero.
  • Salt and sodium are kept as separate fields.
DATA QUALITY

What the numbers can and cannot tell you

Open Food Facts is community contributed, so coverage and accuracy vary by product, brand and country. A product may have an image or ingredient list but no nutrition panel. Source records can also be incomplete or out of date.

An absent allergen or trace statement is not proof that a product is allergen-free or safe for a person with an allergy. Check the package and consult the manufacturer when needed. LabelLens provides informational comparisons, not medical advice or an overall health score.

For current product details, review the package and open the linked source record from the product page.
